# Passenger details and bookings

Section 09 / 0.10.0. Passengers give their details with their seats. They cannot pay or receive a ticket yet.

## For a passenger

1. Choose seats on a trip, as in [seat holds](SEAT_HOLDS.md). A form appears for the seats you picked.
2. Enter a full name for each seat and one mobile number for the booking. A local Sudanese number or an international one both work, in Arabic or Latin digits. No account, age or date of birth is needed. A child with a seat is simply named on it.
3. Choose Hold these seats. Your seats and details are saved together or not at all.
4. Keep the booking reference.

To reopen a booking, choose My booking. The device that booked opens it directly from the list there. On any other device, enter the reference and the mobile number. That view shows a masked number and shortened names, and lets you see or release the booking but not change it. Five wrong attempts on one reference block it for 15 minutes. Forget removes the link from a shared device and does not cancel anything.

## For a company

Open Bookings in the company workspace. This needs permission to view bookings and passenger details. Search by reference, mobile number or part of a traveler's name, or open a booking to see its travelers and history. The trip's Seats and holds view also names travelers. Every list, search, seat view and opened booking is recorded in the company audit, without the search text.

While seats are held, staff with permission to manage bookings can correct details, giving a reason and their current password. The old values are not kept. Do not put names or numbers in a reason.

## Erasure

Names and numbers, and staff-written reasons, are erased automatically 90 days after the trip ends, whatever happened to the booking. The reference, seats, amounts, status and history stay. An erased booking can no longer be opened by reference and mobile. Local backups are not erased: delete old backups so they do not keep details past that date.
