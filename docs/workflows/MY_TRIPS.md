# Passenger My Trips

## Book

Use Book to select the existing origin, destination, date and 1-10 passenger intent. Search uses published routes and availability. Open the real seat map, choose one seat per traveller, enter details and hold the selected seats using the existing transaction. The selected seats and server response determine the reservation; no seat is locked just by changing the search count. Payment remains at the company station.

## Trips

Upcoming and Past read bookings whose device keys already exist on this browser/app. Exact departure/server timestamps determine chronology; cancelled/released/refunded/expired records are shown in history. Records with identical departure times have stable ordering, with confirmed tickets first. At most 50 saved bookings are fetched at once; the existing reference/mobile form can find another booking. Refresh trips rechecks server state. Load failures are shown rather than presented as an empty history.

The next booking has a large route card, real endpoints, date/time and a text status. The vehicle image is labeled Route illustration and does not identify the assigned bus. Journey details show the booked pickup/drop-off city or station and local times; unknown details are explicitly marked. Contact the company to confirm any missing pickup point.

View ticket opens the existing booking route and scrolls to the ticket without reloading a loaded ticket for a fragment-only change. A valid paid device-owned booking shows its seat numbers and genuine QR from the existing ticket credential, rendered as sharp black/white vector geometry. The driver still verifies validity and assignment online. Unpaid, refunded or cancelled bookings never receive a usable QR presentation.

Another device can use reference and mobile for the existing masked view; that proof does not grant QR credentials or persist a passenger account. Use the company-issued ticket number/copy when QR is unavailable. Forget removes the local key/route cache link without cancelling the server booking. No passenger names, phone numbers or QR credentials are copied into the route cache.

## Profile and accessibility

Profile honestly identifies a guest travel space with appearance/language and privacy/support controls, not a fabricated signed-in account. Arabic/English, RTL/LTR, light/dark themes and reduced motion are supported. The frosted bottom bar provides Book, Trips and Profile; QR geometry stays opaque and sharp, including print.
