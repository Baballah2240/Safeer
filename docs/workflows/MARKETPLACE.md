# Marketplace and company pages

Section 07 / 0.8.0. Passengers can find trips and companies. They cannot book yet.

## For a company

1. Open your company, then Public page, and choose Edit public page. This needs permission to manage the company profile.
2. Add a description in English and Arabic and the phone, email and address passengers should use. These are separate from your private company contact details.
3. Tick List this company for passengers and save with your current password.
4. The page appears once the company is approved. Only trips you have published, with a future departure, are shown. Untick the box to hide everything at once.

Passengers never see your drivers, staff, bus registrations, bus codes, draft trips or private contact details.

## For a passenger

Open /marketplace with no account. Choose a departure city, destination and date, then sort by earliest departure, lowest price or shortest journey. Open a company to see its details, routes, bus types and upcoming trips. Search matches where a trip starts and ends; other stops are listed on each trip. Contact the company to travel until booking opens in Section 08.
