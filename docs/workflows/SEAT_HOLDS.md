# Seat holds

Section 08 / 0.9.0. Passengers can hold seats for a limited time. They cannot pay or receive a ticket yet.

## For a passenger

1. Open /marketplace, find a trip and choose Select seats. No account or personal details are needed.
2. Choose up to 10 seats. The map shows available, selected and taken seats and refreshes every 15 seconds.
3. Choose Hold these seats. All of them are held, or none are if one has just been taken.
4. Keep the booking reference. The countdown shows how long the seats stay held. The company sets this time; a hold always ends by departure.
5. Pay at a company station before the time runs out. Station payment arrives in Section 10, so today a hold ends by running out or being released.

The hold page reopens only on the device that made it. Anyone else with the link sees a message asking them to contact the company. If the reference is lost on that device, the company can still find the hold on the trip's seat view.

## For a company

Open a trip and choose Seats and holds. This needs permission to view held seats. The map shows which seats are held, and the list shows each hold's reference, seats, end time and history. Live holds are always listed, followed by the newest closed ones.

To free seats early, choose Release hold, give a reason and enter your current password. This needs permission to manage seat holds. Existing company roles need these two permissions added; new companies get them on the company administrator role.

## What protects the seats

- A trip's seats can be held only while it is published, in the future, and its company is approved and listed.
- Changing to a bus with a different seat map is refused while seats are held. A bus with the same seat map keeps its holds.
- Canceling a trip releases its holds at once, and the passenger's page says so.
- History is kept: a hold can be closed once, and nothing about it can be edited afterwards.
