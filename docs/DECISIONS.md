Implementation boundaries: boarding opens 24 hours before scheduled departure and closes at actual departure or scheduled arrival, whichever comes first; departure cannot be recorded before scheduled departure; arrival follows actual departure. They require authenticated assigned-trip access and never independently grant boarding or manifest access. No camera permission or offline boarding: use a keyboard scanner or paste a QR value. Incident notes are bounded, idempotent and cleared 90 days after scheduled arrival alongside passenger erasure. No passenger mobile app, external connectivity or Section 13 financial reporting is implemented here.

## 2026-11-02 - Section 11 authorization

D33: The owner's latest "next" approves Section 11: passenger luggage. The owner chose: (1) staff record weight only, with no allowance or fees; (2) collection is checked against the ticket number or the booking's mobile number; (3) any luggage staff can report an exception, resolving one takes a separate permission with an outcome, and an open exception blocks collection; (4) bags can be registered only on paid tickets, and a ticket cannot be refunded while its bags are with the company, so staff return them first with a reason. Resolving a missing bag as settled or closed marks it lost, which ends custody and allows the refund. A bag cannot be marked in transit before the trip's departure time or moved forward on a canceled trip. The ticket number is left off the tag, because it alone can collect the bag.

## 2026-11-02 - Section 10 authorization

D32: The owner's latest "next" approves Section 10: station ticketing, payments, tickets and receipts. The owner chose: (1) A paid passenger cancellation gets a full refund until departure and nothing after; a canceled trip always gets a full refund. There are no fees or company cutoffs. (2) Any ticketing employee can refund, with a reason and their current password; there is no separate approval. (3) Cash is always available, and each company can enable bank transfer and mobile wallet, which need a typed transaction reference and connect to nothing. Only cash counts toward the drawer. (4) A hold that has ended can be paid late only by someone with the new late-payment permission, with a reason, if the seats are still free and the seat map has not changed. Every payment and refund needs the person's open session at a counter. Implementation decisions: payment is for the full amount only, because partial and split payment are not approved, so one payment settles one booking and payment_allocations is not created. A refund goes out by the method it came in. Payment, receipt, tickets and seat confirmation commit together, and a repeated key returns the original. Expected cash comes directly from the permanent payment and refund records, so cash_movements is not created. Closing a session keeps any difference and requires a note. A walk-in sale holds and pays in one transaction and works for unlisted companies. Later prints are marked as copies. Tickets carry a number but no QR code until Section 12 defines scanning. Station-scoped staff can find any of their company's bookings by reference or full mobile number, because a passenger may pay at any station, but cannot list them or search by name. A trip cannot be canceled once it has arrived, so refunds are not reopened afterwards. Session notes are cleared 90 days after closing. Names on tickets follow the 90-day erasure, while financial records are kept. Final receipt wording, tax and legal retention are not decided and need review before real use.

## 2026-11-02 - Section 08 authorization

D30: The owner's latest "next" approves Section 08: seat inventory and temporary reservations. Decisions: (1) Passengers hold seats with no account and no personal details; names and phone numbers belong to Section 09. (2) A hold takes 1-10 seats, all or none, for the company's hold minutes, capped at departure. (3) Claims live in one seat_allocations table with a stop range and a database exclusion constraint; holds cover the whole trip now, and segment sales stay disabled because fares cover the whole trip (D28). This replaces the planned separate trip_seat_inventory and seat_holds tables: the trip row is the lock, which is simpler and just as safe for whole-trip holds. (4) Expiry is decided from expires_at against the database clock at every read and write; a 30-second background job only tidies the records. (5) The browser creates a random key per hold attempt. It makes retries idempotent and is the only way to reopen or release the hold; only its hash is stored, and the reference alone opens nothing. Losing the key does not lose the seats. (6) Anonymous hold writes are the first without a session, so they require the page origin and JSON and are limited to 120 seats per client address per 15 minutes; hosting must forward the real client address before public exposure. (7) A bus change to a different seat map is refused while seats are held. Canceling a trip releases its holds. (8) Staff need the new company.bookings.read/manage permissions to see held seats and release a hold with a reason and their current password. Existing company role copies are not rewritten. (9) Station payment and confirmation stay in Section 10, so a hold cannot yet become a ticket.
