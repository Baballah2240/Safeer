## 2026-11-02 - Section 06 authorization

D27: The owner's latest "next" approves Section 06: trip scheduling, bus and driver assignment, stop and seat-layout snapshots and publication. Use company-wide company.trips.read/manage; managing also needs company-wide route, fleet and driver reads, and support grants gain no trip access. New-company administrator templates gain both permissions; existing company role copies remain unchanged. A trip copies its route code, names and stops when created, so later route edits cannot change it, and starts as a draft. Times use the company timezone, and each stop stores whole minutes after departure, strictly increasing from 0, up to 7 days. One request may create 1-31 trips, all or none. Status moves only draft to published, and draft or published to canceled with a required reason; canceled is final. Publication requires an approved company, an active assigned bus and driver, a fare above zero and a future departure. Published trips keep their time and fare: cancel and reschedule to change them. Each trip stores its fare and currency (default SDG) in minor units as a snapshot; no fare rules, sales or payments exist. One bus or driver cannot overlap two live trips. Replacing either keeps the earlier assignment and requires a reason, and each assignment snapshots the bus, seat-layout revision, seat count and driver name. No seats are sold yet, so reassigning a published trip is unrestricted here; Section 08 must add its own rule once reservations exist. Active assignments protect their bus and driver from deactivation. Trip readers see the driver's name but never contact details. Bound each company to 5000 trips. Nothing is deleted. The passenger marketplace, seat inventory, bookings, departure and arrival records, driver logins, payments and freight remain deferred. Back up and restore-verify the local upgrade; archive and stop before Section 07.

## 2026-11-02 - Section 04 authorization

Bound the local catalog to 1000 cities and 500 routes/company; keep history through deactivation, not deletion. Verify database constraints/RLS, concurrency, migrations, scope/revocation, bilingual responsive UI and existing workflows. Back up and verify the local upgrade, archive the completed source, and stop before Section 05. No fleet, trips, fares, real passenger bookings, freight or payments are authorized by this section.
