About Safeer

One platform for the road between cities.

Safeer brings approved Sudanese bus companies together in one place, so you can find a trip, choose your seat and send a parcel without the guesswork.

The companies run the buses and the stations. Safeer keeps every booking, ticket and parcel organized and easy to follow.

  • Safe

    Only approved bus companies, and every ticket is checked at boarding.

  • Fast

    Find a trip and hold your seats in a few taps, on any phone.

  • Organized

    Clear fares, receipts and tracking codes for every booking and parcel.

  • Welcoming

    Arabic and English, and no account needed to book.

Passenger travel

Comfortable seats, clear plans.

Compare published trips between cities, see the fare before you commit and pick the seats you want.

  • Choose your seat

    See the seat map and pick a seat for every traveler.

  • Know the fare first

    Every trip shows its price before you hold a seat.

  • Hold now, pay at the station

    Your seats are held for a set time while you pay the company in person.

  • QR ticket for boarding

    After payment your ticket appears on your phone, and the driver checks it as you board.

  • Luggage, tagged

    Declare your bags when you book, and the station tags them for the trip.

  • Book as a guest

    No account needed. Keep your booking reference to find it again.

City-to-city shipping

Send parcels on the buses you trust.

Drop your parcel at the bus company’s office. It travels on a scheduled trip and waits safely at the destination office until the right person collects it.

  • Simple, fixed prices

    One price per item type: envelope, small parcel, large parcel or other item.

  • Pay once at drop-off

    You get a printed receipt with your tracking code.

  • Track every step

    Follow it from received to on the bus, arrived and collected.

  • Only the right receiver

    The receiver’s name and phone number must match what you gave.

  • Private by design

    Public tracking shows the status and offices only, never names or numbers.

Not accepted: cash, weapons, hazardous goods, live animals or perishable food.

How it works

Three simple steps.

  1. 01

    Find your trip

    Choose your route and date, compare trips and pick your seats.

  2. 02

    Hold and pay

    Your seats are held while you pay at the company’s station.

  3. 03

    Board and go

    Show your QR ticket to the driver and enjoy the ride.

  1. 01

    Drop it off

    Bring your parcel to the company’s office with the receiver’s name and phone number.

  2. 02

    It travels by bus

    Pay the fixed price, keep your tracking code and follow its journey.

  3. 03

    Collected safely

    The receiver shows matching details at the destination office.

Why Safeer

Built for peace of mind.

Every part of Safeer is designed so you always know what happens next.

  • Approved companies only

    Bus companies appear on Safeer only after they are approved.

  • Prices you see upfront

    Trip fares and parcel prices are shown before you pay.
